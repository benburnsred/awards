Before you vote

Enter your name. It's used for every category, and each name can vote only once. You'll vote in every category, then submit them all together.

Is Discord sign-in safe?

Yes. Signing in is optional; you can always vote with just a name.

🔒 What we do

  • You sign in on the real discord.com. This site never sees your password.
  • We only receive your username and profile picture. Discord shows you this before you click Authorize.
  • We can't read your messages, see your servers or friends, get your email, or post as you.
  • Your ballot stores a scrambled code instead of your Discord ID, so your votes stay private.
  • You can still change the name shown on your ballot before voting.

🛡️ Stay safe from fakes

  • Only sign in when the address bar says discord.com.
  • Admins will never DM you asking you to log in, or for codes or passwords. If someone does, report it.

🔌 Remove access later

Discord → User Settings → Authorized Apps → Anime X Angas Awards → Deauthorize.
